About Our Enterprise

Trusted Manufacturers for Girls’ Fashion Architecture

Aay Vee International is a premier Ludhiana-based clothing industry leader producing elite lines of girls' clothing wear under the flagship brand Real Baby. With over three decades of manufacturing experience, we merge quality textiles with high-speed machinery.

Our operation processes advanced textiles—ranging from high-elasticity Hosiery Fabrics, woven Textile Fabrics, heavy-duty Denim, specialized Washing Fabrics, to premium Imported structural cloths—ensuring highly competitive retail performance.
